Meet the **Factory**: queue backlog items and get draft pull requests back. Press **Run in Factory** on a backlog item, or tick several and press **Run selected in Factory**, then **Start** on the project's new **Factory** tab. Each item runs through your review workflow in its own worktree, and when the agents approve the change, Warpforge commits it, pushes it and opens a draft pull request for you to review — linked to the item, with `Closes #N` for a GitHub issue. Merging it marks the item done; closing it sends the item back to the backlog. Limits on runs at once, open pull requests, items per day, quota headroom and free disk keep unattended work in check, and every run shows its outcome and reported cost. Agents can queue items and read the queue too.
