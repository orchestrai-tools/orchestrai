Agents can now use the in-app browser themselves. Ask one to open your app, click through a flow, fill in a form or read the page's console errors, and it works in its own Agent tab in the Browser, next to yours — reading the page, clicking, typing, taking screenshots and checking the console as it goes, so you can watch it and your own tabs are left alone. When the Browser pane isn't open, pages load in the background without taking over your screen. Your project's own dev services are open to the agent right away; any other site asks you first in the task's chat, because the browser is signed in as you — allow it once, for the rest of the task, or deny it.
