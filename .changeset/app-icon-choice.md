OrchestrAI has a new conductor icon, and you can pick the one you like. Open Settings › Appearance (Command-,) and choose Silhouette, Maestro, Line, or AI under App icon. Your choice shows in the Dock and the app switcher right away and stays after a restart. Finder and Launchpad keep the Silhouette.
