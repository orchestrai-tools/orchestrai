OrchestrAI now has its own name, settings, and data, so it runs side by side with Warpforge on the same Mac without the two getting in each other's way. Your projects, task history, and memory now live in OrchestrAI's own folder, and each project's settings live in an `.orchestrai` folder inside the repository. Nothing carries over from Warpforge: add your projects again, and copy a repository's `.warpforge` folder to `.orchestrai` to keep its services and workflows. Automatic updates are turned off for now, until OrchestrAI publishes its own signed releases.
