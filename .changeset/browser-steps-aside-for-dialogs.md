The in-app browser no longer covers dialogs, menus and notifications. Opening settings, the push dialog or quick open now shows it on top and lets you click it, and the page comes back exactly where you left it once the dialog closes. A menu or notification that overlaps the page makes the page step aside for it instead of hiding it. Switching tasks or tabs and returning to the browser also keeps each page as it was, with no reload, and tabs keep their titles and back history.
