The in-app browser no longer covers dialogs, menus and notifications. Opening settings, the push dialog, quick open or any menu that overlaps the page now shows it on top and lets you click it, and the page comes back exactly where you left it once the overlay closes. Switching tasks or tabs and returning to the browser also keeps each page as it was, with no reload, and tabs keep their titles and back history.
