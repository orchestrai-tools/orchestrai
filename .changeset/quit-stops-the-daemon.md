Quitting Warpforge now quits it. Closing the window, pressing ⌘Q or choosing Quit from the Dock menu stops the agents, services and port-forwards Warpforge started and shuts down the local engine behind the app — after one prompt that lists what is running, with a Wait button if you are not ready. A background engine you started yourself from the CLI is left untouched, and your conversations and task history are kept exactly as before.
